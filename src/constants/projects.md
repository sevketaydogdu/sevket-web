Easy Dhikr: ZikirMatik is a dedicated mobile app that helps you manage your dhikr in a practical way. The app provides an automatic counter, freeing you from the constant need to count while doing dhikr.

If you have completed a dhikr list or wish to reset it, you can easily delete it by donating the list. This flexibility allows users to restart or modify their dhikr lists whenever they want.

Easy Dhikr: ZikirMatik prioritizes user privacy. The app does not request personal information and securely stores user data, ensuring that your dhikr experience remains entirely private. The app is an ideal solution for those with busy lifestyles, individuals looking to regularly track their dhikr, or those who want to manage their dhikr in a more systematic way.

With Easy Dhikr: ZikirMatik, managing your dhikr is now easier and more convenient. Create your own dhikr lists, track your dhikr with the auto-save feature, and delete your dhikr whenever you like. Download this user-friendly app that accompanies you on your spiritual journey and experience the convenience of organizing your dhikr.
